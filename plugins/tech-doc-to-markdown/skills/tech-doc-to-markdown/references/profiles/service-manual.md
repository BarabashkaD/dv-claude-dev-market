# Profile: service-manual

## Signals
- "Service Manual", "Maintenance Manual", "Repair", "Schematic Diagrams", "Parts List", "Adjustment Procedure", "Theory of Operation".
- Fold-out schematics, exploded views, board layouts, voltage/waveform tables at test points.

## Typical content
- Theory of operation by circuit block; block diagrams; full schematics with reference designators and net names.
- Adjustment and alignment procedures with test points, instruments and tolerances.
- Troubleshooting trees, waveform photos at test points, parts lists with part numbers, disassembly steps, exploded views.

## Custom steps
- **Stage 2:** ask which boards or blocks matter; fold-out schematics can be very large, so plan crops per region.
- **Stage 3:** render schematic pages at higher DPI (300+) and tile them into overlapping crops; keep a tile map.
- **Stage 4:** schematics become component tables, net lists and block descriptions (representation guide section 7); keep reference designators exactly. Test-point voltage and waveform tables stay tables; oscilloscope photos get a description plus a table of amplitude, period and timebase.
- **Stage 4:** exploded views become a numbered parts table (item, part number, description, quantity); keep the drawing only if it is a genuine picture needed for assembly, following the photo rule.
- **Stage 5:** check that every reference designator in the parts list appears in a net list or component table, and vice versa.
- **Stage 8:** test-point index, adjustment summary, troubleshooting quick table.

## Questions to ask
- Which boards, assemblies or chapters are needed?
- Should exploded views and board photos be kept as images, or only their parts tables?

## Split suggestion
- Theory of operation; one part per board or schematic sheet; adjustments; troubleshooting; parts lists; disassembly.

## Useful extras
- Test-point index; component-to-sheet map; adjustment checklist; parts cross-reference.
